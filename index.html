Curious about what happens underneath.

I'm a software engineer interested in understanding how systems work from the inside out. I work primarily across backend engineering, Web3 infrastructure, and smart contract security, with experience across both EVM and Solana.

I enjoy working close to the underlying systems: designing APIs, interacting with protocols, writing smart contracts, analyzing vulnerabilities, and building tools that automate difficult technical workflows.

My foundation is in Electrical Engineering, which still shapes how I approach software: as systems of constraints, signals, and trade-offs.
